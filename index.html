Meet Katie

Why I wanted to bring sound into everyday life.

Hi, I’m Katie. I’ve been teaching yoga for over ten years, and my work has grown to include meditation, sound baths and retreats. Through these experiences, I’ve seen how simply taking time to listen can create a welcome pause in a busy day.

Harmonic Rest grew from a simple wish: to bring that invitation to slow down beyond the studio and into everyday life, through original sound experiences you can enjoy wherever you are.

The Three-Minute Reset is a little place to begin. No preparation or expectations — just a few minutes to listen and notice how it feels for you.

Katie Hailey
